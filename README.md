# -Retail-Sales-Performance-Project
An interactive Power BI retail sales dashboard that analyzes product performance, sales channels, and regional trends. It helps visualize key business metrics like total sales, quantity, and customer payment behavior for better decision-making.
📊 Retail Sales Performance Dashboard — Power BI Project
📌 Project Overview

This project presents an interactive Retail Sales Dashboard built in Power BI to analyze product performance, sales channels, payment behavior, and regional sales distribution.

The dashboard transforms raw transactional data into meaningful insights that help understand business trends, customer purchasing behavior, and product demand.

🎯 Objectives

Track overall sales and quantity performance

Compare sales across different sales channels

Analyze payment method usage

Identify top-performing products

Understand regional sales distribution

Enable interactive business exploration

📂 Dataset Description

The dataset contains structured retail sales information, including:

Product details

Sales amount

Quantity sold

Unit price

Sales channel (Store / Online / Direct)

Payment type

State-wise sales records

This data enables multi-dimensional business analysis.

📈 Dashboard Features

🔹 KPI Summary Cards

Total Sales

Total Quantity Sold

These provide a quick snapshot of overall performance.

🔹 Sales by Channel (Pie Chart)

Visualizes how sales are distributed across Store, Online, and Direct channels.

🔹 Sales by Payment Type (Donut Chart)

Shows customer payment preferences such as cash, UPI, credit, and debit cards.

🔹 Quantity by Product (Bar Chart)

Identifies which products have the highest demand.

🔹 Sales by Product (Column Chart)

Highlights top revenue-generating products.

🔹 Average Unit Price by Product

Compares pricing trends across products.

🔹 State-wise Sales (Bar Chart)

Displays regional sales performance to identify strong markets.

🔹 Interactive Filters (Slicers)

Product filter

State filter

Users can dynamically explore the dashboard.

💡 Key Insights

Sales distribution varies significantly across channels

Digital payments play a strong role in customer behavior

Certain products drive both high sales and quantity

Regional markets contribute differently to revenue
